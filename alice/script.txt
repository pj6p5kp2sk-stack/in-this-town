In this town, the last game isn't played with guns. It's played with a cup of tea. But first, Arisu had to survive the face cards. The King of Spades walked through the streets with a giant gun, hunting every player he could find. The Jack of Hearts locked people in a prison, where your life depended on a collar you couldn't see and the lies of everyone around you. The King of Clubs made teams fight for points until only one team was left. Usagi almost gave up. Arisu almost lost his mind. But they kept going, together. At the very end, only one card was left. The Queen of Hearts. Her name was Mira, and her game was croquet. Just croquet, and tea. But while they played, Mira told Arisu a terrible story. None of this was real. There was no borderland. He was making it all up in his head. Arisu started to believe her. Then he remembered Karube, Chota, and everyone who died to keep him alive, and he refused to quit. He won. And then, he opened his eyes in a hospital bed. A meteor had crashed into Shibuya, and everyone in the borderland had been stuck between life and death. Arisu and Usagi survived, and they finally met in the real world. But somewhere, on a table full of cards, one card was still left. The Joker. ///
