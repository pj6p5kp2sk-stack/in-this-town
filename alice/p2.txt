In this town, the safest place to live is a hotel called the Beach. At least, that's what everyone thinks. Arisu arrived with Usagi, a mountain climber who had lost everything too. The Beach was full of pool parties, loud music and hundreds of players in swimsuits, all following one rule. Give every card you win to the Hatter. The Hatter promised that if they collected the whole deck, from ace to king, they could all go home. But the Beach was hiding things. Chishiya, a quiet genius in a white hoodie, was planning to steal the cards. A group with guns wanted to take control. And one night, a girl was found dead in her room. Suddenly, the whole hotel became a game. Find the killer before time runs out, or everyone dies. Friends turned on friends, guns came out, and the Beach burned. Arisu solved the game, but the dream of going home was gone. Then, one by one, every number card in Tokyo was cleared. Giant balloons rose over the city, carrying new cards. Jacks, queens and kings. That was the real twist. Collecting the cards was never the way out. It was only the first level. The face cards were not players. They were the people who live in the borderland and make the rules. And to go home, Arisu would have to beat every single one of them.
