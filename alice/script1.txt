In this town, if your visa runs out, a red laser falls from the sky and kills you. Arisu didn't know that yet. He was just a lazy gamer, standing in the middle of Shibuya with his two best friends, Karube and Chota. Then, for a few seconds, the whole city went dark. When the lights came back on, every single person in Tokyo was gone. No cars. No phones. No people. That night, a glowing screen invited them to play a game. Every game had a playing card. Spades were physical. Clubs were teamwork. Diamonds were brains. And hearts were the worst, because hearts play with your feelings. Win, and you get a visa, a few more days to live. Lose, and the laser finds you. Arisu was good at games, so for a while, they kept winning. Then they walked into a dark garden, and the card on the screen was a heart. The game was hide and seek. And then they heard the last rule. Only one of you can survive. // Arisu looked at his friends. His friends looked at him. Karube and Chota gave their lives, so Arisu could live. Alone and broken, Arisu wanted to give up. Until someone told him about a place where players had a plan to go home. The Beach. ///
