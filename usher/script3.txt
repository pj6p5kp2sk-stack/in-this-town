In this world, the last Usher kids never stood a chance. Tamerlane is haunted by her own mirrors, until she can't tell what's real anymore. Victorine can't stop hearing a heartbeat. Frederick ends up tied down under a giant swinging blade. Then Roderick invites Auguste Dupin, the lawyer who spent years trying to stop him, to his old family house, and tells him the whole truth. In the end, the twins die together, and the House of Usher falls apart. Because when you make a deal with Verna, she always collects.
