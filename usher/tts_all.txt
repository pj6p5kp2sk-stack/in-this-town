In this world, if you make a deal with a mysterious woman named Verna, you get everything you want. Roderick Usher and his twin sister Madeline made that deal on New Year's Eve, nineteen seventy-nine. They became billionaires, running a giant drug company that made people sick while they got rich. But there was one price. Their whole bloodline would end with them, and nobody could ever punish them while they lived. Years later, Roderick has six grown-up children. And in just two weeks, every single one of them is going to die. In this world, the Usher kids start dying in the strangest ways. First, Prospero, the youngest, throws a wild secret party in an old abandoned building, and acid pours down from the sprinklers. Next, Camille sneaks into her sister's lab at night to spy on her, and gets attacked by an angry chimpanzee. Then Leo is haunted by a black cat that just won't die, no matter what he does. And every time, the same woman shows up right before it happens. Verna. Three kids are gone. Three are left. In this world, the last Usher kids never stood a chance. Tamerlane is haunted by her own mirrors, until she can't tell what's real anymore. Victorine can't stop hearing a heartbeat. Frederick ends up tied down under a giant swinging blade. Then Roderick invites Auguste Dupin, the lawyer who spent years trying to stop him, to his old family house, and tells him the whole truth. In the end, the twins die together, and the House of Usher falls apart. Because when you make a deal with Verna, she always collects.
