In this world, the Usher kids start dying in the strangest ways. First, Prospero, the youngest, throws a wild secret party in an old abandoned building, and acid pours down from the sprinklers. Next, Camille sneaks into her sister's lab at night to spy on her, and gets attacked by an angry chimpanzee. Then Leo is haunted by a black cat that just won't die, no matter what he does. And every time, the same woman shows up right before it happens. Verna. Three kids are gone. Three are left.
