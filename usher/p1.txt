In this world, if you make a deal with a mysterious woman named Verna, you get everything you want. Roderick Usher and his twin sister Madeline made that deal on New Year's Eve, nineteen seventy-nine. They became billionaires, running a giant drug company that made people sick while they got rich. But there was one price. Their whole bloodline would end with them, and nobody could ever punish them while they lived. Years later, Roderick has six grown-up children. And in just two weeks, every single one of them is going to die.
